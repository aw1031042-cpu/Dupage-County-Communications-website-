Welcome to DU-COMM

Serving DuPage County with professional emergency communications since 1978.

We are the primary public safety answering point for DuPage County, Illinois. Our trained dispatchers provide critical emergency response services for police, fire, and medical emergencies across the county.

About Us

DuPage County Communications (DU-COMM) is a comprehensive public safety answering point (PSAP) serving all of DuPage County. We handle emergency calls for 911, dispatch police, fire, and EMS services, and maintain critical communication links between first responders and the public.

Our mission is to provide prompt, professional, and compassionate emergency communication services while supporting the safety of our community and the effectiveness of our first responder agencies.

What We Do

  • Answer emergency 911 calls
  • Dispatch police, fire, and EMS resources
  • Maintain county-wide communication systems
  • Provide critical information to first responders
  • Support mutual aid between agencies
  • Maintain public safety radio systems
